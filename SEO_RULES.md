# Top Agro — SEO Rules

SEO must improve discoverability without harming clarity, performance, or factual accuracy.

## 1. General principles

- Human usefulness comes first.
- One page should have one clear primary search intent.
- Avoid duplicate thin pages.
- Do not keyword-stuff.
- Do not generate hundreds of near-identical location/product pages.
- Never invent data to create "SEO content".

---

## 2. Planned URL architecture

Preferred future patterns:

- `/`
- `/produtos`
- `/produtos/[categoria]`
- `/produtos/[categoria]/[marca-modelo]`
- `/usados`
- `/usados/[slug]`
- `/blog`
- `/blog/[slug]`

Keep URLs:
- Lowercase.
- Short.
- Stable.
- Hyphen-separated.
- Without unnecessary IDs when a stable slug works.

Do not change existing indexed URLs without a redirect plan.

---

## 3. Category architecture

Create category pages when they represent a real customer concept.

Examples:
- Ensacadeiras
- Ensiladeiras
- Misturadores de ração
- Distribuidores
- Guinchos
- Grades
- Silos

Do not create a page for every tiny attribute only to target keywords.

---

## 4. Product pages

Each real model can have a canonical detail page when there is enough unique information.

Include:
- Unique title.
- Unique meta description.
- H1 matching real product identity.
- Real images.
- Verified specifications.
- Useful copy.
- Internal links.
- CTA.

Avoid manufacturer-description copy-paste when Top Agro can add useful original context.

---

## 5. Metadata

Every indexable page should have:
- Unique `<title>`.
- Useful meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph title/description/image when relevant.
- Twitter/X card metadata when useful.

Title should lead with what users seek.

Avoid:
`Home | Top Agro`

Prefer something descriptive of the actual page intent.

Do not stuff city/state keywords into every title.

---

## 6. Headings

Use semantic hierarchy:
- One primary H1 per main page.
- H2 for major sections.
- H3 for subsections/cards where semantically appropriate.

Do not choose heading tags based only on font size.

---

## 7. Structured data

When the required factual data exists, consider appropriate Schema.org:
- `Organization`
- `LocalBusiness` where accurate.
- `Product`
- `Offer` only when actual price/availability is public and current.
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `Article` for blog posts.
- `VideoObject` for meaningful product video pages when data exists.

Never output fake:
- rating,
- review count,
- price,
- stock,
- offer expiration,
- SKU.

Validate structured data before shipping.

---

## 8. Local SEO

Top Agro has physical presence in Rio Grande do Sul.

Local pages/details should use verified:
- Business name.
- Address.
- Phone.
- Opening hours.
- Map/location data.
- Store-specific information.

Do not create doorway pages for dozens of cities where Top Agro has no physical location merely to rank.

Regional sales pages may exist when they provide genuinely useful regional content.

---

## 9. Images

For indexable product images:
- Use descriptive filenames when practical.
- Use correct dimensions.
- Compress appropriately.
- Provide meaningful alt text.
- Avoid huge original images when a responsive derivative can be served.

Do not use alt text for keyword stuffing.

---

## 10. Internal linking

Useful links:
- Home → major categories.
- Category → models.
- Model → category.
- Blog → relevant product/category.
- Product → related product only when relevant.
- Used listing → related category/new alternative where useful.

Anchor text should describe the destination.

Avoid dozens of repetitive footer SEO links.

---

## 11. Indexation

Do not index:
- Internal search result pages with thin/duplicate content.
- Staging/preview environments.
- Empty future categories.
- Duplicate filtered URLs.
- Private/admin pages.

Ensure production has correct robots and sitemap behavior before launch.

---

## 12. Performance and Core Web Vitals

SEO and UX share performance goals.

Prioritize:
- Responsive images.
- Correct image dimensions.
- Minimal blocking JS.
- Stable layout.
- Efficient fonts.
- Lazy loading below the fold.
- Avoid large autoplay video.
- Avoid unnecessary third-party scripts.

Do not add a heavy SEO/analytics package when native metadata is sufficient.

---

## 13. Mobile

Google discovery and real Top Agro traffic both make mobile quality critical.

Ensure:
- Same meaningful content as desktop.
- No mobile-only hidden critical copy.
- Readable text.
- Correct viewport.
- Fast loading.
- Accessible tap targets.

---

## 14. Content quality

A product page should be useful even if Google did not exist.

Do not create:
- AI filler.
- Rewritten manufacturer copy with no added value.
- Repetitive city pages.
- Fake FAQs.
- Fake customer questions.

Useful FAQs must reflect real buyer questions and approved answers.

---

## 15. Migration to ecommerce

When ecommerce is introduced:
- Preserve canonical product URLs where possible.
- Add real Offer data only when current.
- Avoid creating a second duplicate ecommerce URL for the same product.
- Plan redirects before changing slugs.
- Maintain analytics continuity.

SEO architecture should support future commerce, not force a future replatform.
