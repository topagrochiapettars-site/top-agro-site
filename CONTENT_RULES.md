# Top Agro — Content Rules

## 1. Language

Customer-facing site copy:
- Brazilian Portuguese.
- Direct.
- Clear.
- Agricultural/rural vocabulary when customers actually use it.
- Professional without corporate jargon.

Do not write like a software company.

Avoid inflated language:
- "revolucionário"
- "incomparável"
- "o melhor do mercado"
- "solução definitiva"
unless there is specific approved evidence.

---

## 2. Product truth is mandatory

Every technical claim must come from an approved source:
- Manufacturer material.
- Top Agro internal product data.
- Verified product documentation.
- User-provided specification.
- Approved structured product data.

Never guess a spec because another model/brand has something similar.

If a fact is unknown:
- Omit it.
- Mark it as pending in development.
- Ask the user.

---

## 3. Product naming

Use consistent structure:
`[Product type] [Brand] [Model]`

Examples:
- `Ensacadeira Primus 2 Eixos Gasolina`
- `Rosca Transportadora Metal Agro 150 mm`

Do not change manufacturer/model spelling for style.

Maintain canonical product names for:
- Page title.
- Breadcrumb.
- Product card.
- SEO metadata.
- WhatsApp prefill.

---

## 4. Product page copy structure

Preferred order:

### Short summary
One or two sentences:
- What it is.
- What job it does.
- Who it is for.

### Main benefits
3–6 factual bullets.

### Key specifications
Only decision-relevant facts.

### Applications
Real use cases.

### Important considerations
Compatibility, tractor requirement, power, capacity, material limits, etc. when verified.

### Commercial CTA
Contact Top Agro.

Do not copy the same generic description across every model.

---

## 5. Benefit writing

Convert specs into benefits only when the connection is valid.

Good:
- `Capacidade de até X t/h, ajudando a agilizar o processo de ensacamento.`

Bad:
- `Produza 10x mais` when no evidence exists.

Distinguish:
- Fact.
- Benefit.
- Recommendation.
- Sales condition.

---

## 6. Used machinery content

Every used listing should use verified data when available:
- Brand.
- Model.
- Year.
- Condition.
- Hours/use if known.
- Location.
- Included accessories.
- Recent maintenance if documented.
- Real photos.
- Availability status.

Never hide known material defects.

Never describe a used machine as "nova" or "zero" unless factually true.

---

## 7. Images and captions

Image alt text should describe the real subject, not keyword-stuff.

Good:
`Ensacadeira Primus de dois eixos com motor a gasolina`

Bad:
`melhor ensacadeira silagem preço promoção Top Agro RS Brasil`

Captions should add useful context when needed.

Do not label AI-generated machinery as a real product.

---

## 8. Calls to action

CTA language should match user intent.

Product page:
- `Solicitar cotação`
- `Falar sobre este modelo`

Category:
- `Ver modelos`
- `Falar com um especialista`

Used:
- `Consultar disponibilidade`

Avoid repeating the exact same CTA every 200 pixels.

---

## 9. Blog/content

Educational content should answer real buyer questions.

Useful topics:
- How to choose equipment.
- Capacity differences.
- Application/compatibility.
- Maintenance basics.
- Comparison between equipment types.
- Regional production/use cases when grounded in reliable data.
- Buyer checklists.

Do not create thin SEO articles that repeat product pages.

---

## 10. Regional/agronomic claims

Do not invent state-by-state demand or crop suitability.

When regional content is requested:
- Use supplied/approved data such as IBGE or other reliable sources.
- Separate observed production data from commercial inference.
- Do not imply that production volume automatically equals product demand.

---

## 11. Pricing and commercial conditions

Never expose internal:
- Cost.
- Margin.
- Commission.
- Supplier negotiation.
- Internal freight table.
- Customer-specific discount.
unless explicitly requested for a protected internal tool.

Public website copy should only show approved customer-facing conditions.

---

## 12. Contact information

Store phone, address, opening hours, salesperson routing, and WhatsApp numbers must come from verified project data.

Never invent contact details.

Centralize repeated contact information so it can be updated once.

---

## 13. Claims and proof

If a claim uses:
- percentage,
- time saving,
- payback,
- capacity,
- throughput,
- economy,
- warranty,
- delivery time,
- number of customers,
- number of stores,

there must be an approved source.

Do not turn estimates into guarantees.

Use qualifiers when appropriate:
- `até`
- `aproximadamente`
- `conforme configuração`
- `consulte disponibilidade`

---

## 14. Tone examples

Preferred:
`Precisa escolher entre modelos de 1 e 2 eixos? Fale com a equipe e informe o volume que você pretende ensacar.`

Avoid:
`Descubra a inovação definitiva que vai transformar completamente a sua produção!`

Preferred:
`Consulte disponibilidade e frete para sua região.`

Avoid:
`Corra! Últimas unidades!` unless that stock state is real and current.
