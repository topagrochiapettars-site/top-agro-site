# Top Agro — Conversion Rules

## 1. Current conversion goal

The current website is a lead-generation website.

Primary conversion:
- Start a qualified sales conversation on WhatsApp.

Secondary conversions may include:
- View a product/category.
- Watch a product video.
- View used equipment.
- Call/contact a store.
- Read useful content that supports purchase confidence.

Do not optimize for ecommerce checkout until ecommerce exists.

---

## 2. CTA hierarchy

Use one dominant primary CTA per decision area.

Preferred primary labels:
- `Falar com um especialista`
- `Solicitar cotação`
- `Consultar disponibilidade`
- `Falar no WhatsApp`

Contextual labels:
- `Tenho interesse neste modelo`
- `Quero saber se serve para minha propriedade`
- `Consultar frete`

Avoid generic:
- `Clique aqui`
- `Enviar`
- `Saiba mais` when a more specific label is possible.

Avoid false urgency:
- No fake countdown.
- No fake stock scarcity.
- No fake "X pessoas vendo".
- No fake discount timer.

---

## 3. WhatsApp lead quality

A click is more valuable when the salesperson receives context.

Where possible, prefill:
- Product/category.
- Model.
- Current page.
- Optional campaign/source ID.

Example structure:
`Olá, vim pelo site da Top Agro e quero informações sobre [PRODUTO/MODELO].`

Do not prefill a very long sales script.

---

## 4. First screen conversion

Above the fold should usually contain:
- Clear value proposition.
- Relevant visual.
- One primary CTA.
- Optional supporting proof.

Do not put multiple unrelated offers in the hero.

Do not make the user scroll just to discover what Top Agro does.

---

## 5. Product page conversion sequence

Recommended flow:
1. Product identity.
2. Real image/video.
3. One-sentence use case.
4. Core verified benefits.
5. Key specs.
6. Compatibility/use notes.
7. Trust/logistics information when verified.
8. CTA.
9. Additional technical content.
10. Related products.

The CTA should appear before the user reaches the bottom.

On mobile, consider a compact sticky CTA when it improves usability.

---

## 6. Category page conversion

A category page should help users choose, not merely display inventory.

Include:
- Short category explanation.
- Brand/model groups.
- Filters only when they are genuinely useful.
- Product cards with differentiators.
- CTA for users who do not know which model they need.

Useful fallback CTA:
`Não sabe qual modelo escolher? Fale com um especialista.`

---

## 7. Trust before pressure

Agricultural equipment is a considered purchase.

Increase confidence with:
- Real company presence.
- Real product media.
- Clear specifications.
- Real manufacturer identity.
- Verified service/warranty information.
- Transparent contact process.
- Real delivery/customer evidence.

Do not compensate for weak information with aggressive CTA repetition.

---

## 8. Price handling

Current default:
- Do not display a price unless the user/business explicitly decides that the product should have a public price.

Used equipment:
- Price may be introduced selectively in the future.

Never:
- Invent price.
- Infer price from unrelated conversations.
- Show an outdated price as current.
- Present "starting at" without real source data.

---

## 9. Freight and delivery

Only state delivery/freight promises when verified.

Avoid generic claims like:
- "Entrega para todo o Brasil" unless operationally confirmed for the product.
- "Frete grátis" unless valid for the offer.

When useful, CTA can ask:
`Consultar frete para minha cidade`

---

## 10. Lead friction

Reduce friction:
- Do not require account creation.
- Do not force a form before WhatsApp.
- Do not make users select store/salesperson unless necessary.
- Do not ask repeated questions the page already knows.

If routing by region is implemented, keep the user-facing flow simple.

---

## 11. Measurement

When analytics is explicitly approved, track useful events:
- `view_product`
- `view_category`
- `whatsapp_click`
- `quote_request`
- `used_item_view`
- `phone_click`
- `video_play`

Do not add analytics SDKs or ad pixels without explicit approval.

Event naming should be centralized and documented.

Never send sensitive customer data in analytics event parameters.

---

## 12. A/B testing

Do not create random visual variants without a hypothesis.

A test should define:
- What changes.
- Why it may improve conversion.
- Primary metric.
- Guardrail metric.
- Test duration/sample approach when applicable.

Examples:
- CTA wording.
- Hero proof placement.
- Product-card CTA placement.
- Mobile sticky CTA.

Never test factual product claims that are not verified.

---

## 13. Conversion quality over raw clicks

Do not maximize WhatsApp clicks by making every element a CTA.

A qualified conversation is more valuable than accidental clicks.

Pages should help users understand:
- What the product is.
- Whether it is relevant.
- What to ask next.

The site should reduce wasted sales conversations, not just create more of them.
